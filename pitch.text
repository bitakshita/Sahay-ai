# SAHAY AI
### A Voice-Enabled Multilingual Assistant for Financial and Government Assistance

**The Problem**

Accessing financial guidance and government scheme information can be difficult for people who face language barriers, struggle with complex terminology, or find it inconvenient to navigate multiple websites. Text-only platforms can also be challenging for users who are more comfortable speaking than typing.

**Our Solution**

Sahay AI is an AI-powered multilingual assistant that makes financial and government-related information easier to access through both text and voice interactions.

Users can ask questions in English, Hindi or Hinglish and receive conversational explanations. By combining voice technology, generative AI and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Sahay AI aims to make essential information more accessible and understandable.

**Key Features**

- **Voice Interaction:** Integrates Gnani AI's Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech APIs, allowing users to speak their questions and listen to responses.
- **Multilingual Assistance:** Supports interactions in English, Hindi and Hinglish.
- **AI-Powered Conversations:** Uses Google Gemini to understand questions and generate easy-to-understand explanations.
- **Knowledge-Grounded RAG:** Retrieves relevant information from a curated knowledge base of government schemes and tax-related facts before generating answers.
- **Source Transparency:** Provides source links and available verification dates so users can cross-check information.
- **Financial Tools:** Includes tax estimation and personal transaction tracking features.

**How It Works**

1. The user types or speaks a question.
2. Gnani AI converts spoken input into text.
3. The backend identifies relevant information from the knowledge base using keyword-based retrieval.
4. Google Gemini generates a response using the retrieved context.
5. The answer is displayed and can be converted into speech using Gnani AI's Text-to-Speech API.

**Technology Stack**

- Frontend: React.js and Vite
- Backend: Python and FastAPI
- Database: SQLite
- Speech Technology: Gnani AI APIs (STT and TTS)
- Language Model: Google Gemini
- Retrieval: Custom keyword-based RAG

**Impact and Vision**

Sahay AI aims to reduce language and accessibility barriers when accessing essential financial and government information. Our vision is to make reliable information easier to understand through conversational, multilingual and voice-enabled AI.
